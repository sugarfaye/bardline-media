About Our Studio

Preserving the Ephemeral Power of Live Theatre

Founded under the creative legacy of classical American repertory theatre, Bardline Media operates at the intersection of live performance and cinematic storytelling. We work directly with artistic directors, playwrights, and ensemble casts to translate the intimacy of the proscenium onto digital platforms.

Our multidisciplinary team manages every phase of production logistics—from pre-run blocking consultations and technical multi-cam captures to post-production color grading, spatial audio mixing, and international distribution streaming workflows.

  • ✓ Ultra-low footprint multi-camera setups preserving the live audience experience
  • ✓ Dedicated coordination and licensing workflows with theatrical unions (AEA, SDC, USA829)
  • ✓ Comprehensive educational licensing and high-fidelity institutional archiving

"Theatre exists in the shared breath of the moment. Bardline's mission is not merely to record a performance, but to capture the emotional truth and vital rhythm that makes classical drama endure."

Marcus Vance
Artistic Director & Executive Producer

Our Capabilities

Full-Lifecycle Production Services

From stage floor coordination to final digital delivery, we provide end-to-end production support tailored to performing arts groups.

🎬

Multi-Cam Stage Capture

State-of-the-art 4K/6K cinema cameras deployed on silent motorized rigs, capturing live repertory performances without obstructing patrons.

📋

Production Coordination

Full schedule management, theatrical crew liaison, call sheets, vendor contracts, and logistics handling led by dedicated production coordinators.

🎧

Acoustic & Spatial Audio

Multi-track wireless mic coordination, stage boundary microphone arrays, and Dolby Atmos audio mixing that faithfully replicates auditorium acoustic spatiality.

📜

Rights & Guild Clearance

Standardized negotiation assistance for streaming allowances, archival exemptions, and educational secondary-use waivers across creative unions.

🏛️

Institutional Archiving

LTO tape long-term storage and ProRes master deliverables prepared to Library of Congress and museum digital preservation standards.

🌐

Streaming & Digital Distribution

Secure VOD portal delivery, tokenized subscriber access, and geoblocked distribution pipelines tailored to seasonal member patrons.
